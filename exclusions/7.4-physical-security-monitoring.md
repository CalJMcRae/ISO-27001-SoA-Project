## Control 7.4: Physical Security Monitoring

### Exclusion Statement
This control is NOT APPLICABLE to CloudNative Analytics Ltd.

### Business Context
With no physical premises (see 7.1), there is no location for the company to
monitor via CCTV, guards, or physical intrusion detection. All infrastructure
runs on AWS, whose physical data centers are monitored by AWS itself as part
of its own ISO 27001 and SOC 2 Type II certified control environment.

### Risk Assessment Linkage

| Physical Risk | Alternative Control | Reference |
|---|---|---|
| Physical intrusion into infrastructure hosting customer data | AWS shared responsibility model (SOC 2 Type II) | AWS-SOC2-REVIEW |
| Unauthorized access to company systems | 8.15 Logging, 8.16 Monitoring activities | CloudTrail, SIEM |

### Evidence of Non-Applicability
- AWS SOC 2 Type II report, reviewed annually, confirming AWS's physical
  monitoring controls over its data centers
- Shared responsibility model documentation from AWS delineating physical
  security as an AWS-owned control
- Absence of any company-owned physical location in the asset register

### Auditor Anticipated Questions

Q: "How do you get assurance that AWS's physical monitoring is adequate?"
A: Through annual review of AWS's SOC 2 Type II report and AWS's own ISO 27001
   certification, which is assessed as part of our supplier risk management
   process under control 5.22.

Q: "What about monitoring for company equipment used off-site?"
A: That's addressed by 8.1 (endpoint device management) and 7.9 (security of
   off-premises assets), which use technical rather than physical monitoring —
   MDM location tracking and remote wipe rather than cameras or guards.

### Conditions for Re-evaluation
This exclusion should be re-evaluated if the company opens any physical
premises, or if AWS's shared responsibility posture changes such that
physical monitoring becomes a customer-owned control.
