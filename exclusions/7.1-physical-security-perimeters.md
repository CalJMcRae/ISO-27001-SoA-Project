## Control 7.1: Physical Security Perimeters

### Exclusion Statement
This control is NOT APPLICABLE to CloudNative Analytics Ltd.

### Business Context
CloudNative Analytics has operated as a fully remote organization since its
founding in 2021. The company:
- Leases no office space, in any jurisdiction
- Owns no buildings, server rooms, or storage facilities
- Runs 100% of its infrastructure on AWS (eu-west-1)

There is no physical location under company control to which a "security
perimeter" concept could apply.

### Risk Assessment Linkage

| Physical Risk | Alternative Control | Reference |
|---|---|---|
| Unauthorized physical access to systems | 8.1 User endpoint devices | MDM enrollment, disk encryption |
| Unauthorized access to customer data | 8.3 Information access restriction | AWS IAM, RBAC |
| Loss/theft of company equipment | 7.9 Security of assets off-premises | MDM remote wipe |

### Evidence of Non-Applicability
- Company registration documents showing a registered agent address only, no operational premises
- AWS billing and infrastructure diagrams confirming 100% cloud hosting
- HR policy confirming remote-first employment status for all 85 staff

### Auditor Anticipated Questions

Q: "Does the company rent co-working space anywhere?"
A: No. Employees may occasionally use personal co-working memberships, but these
   are not company-leased, company-controlled, or used to store company assets
   or data.

Q: "What if an employee works from a coffee shop — isn't that a perimeter risk?"
A: That risk is addressed through control 7.7 (clear desk/screen) and endpoint
   controls under 8.1, not through a physical perimeter control, since the
   company has no ownership or control over that space.

### Conditions for Re-evaluation
This exclusion should be re-evaluated if the company leases or acquires any
physical premises, opens a data center, or stores company assets in a
fixed physical location.
