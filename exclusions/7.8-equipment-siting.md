## Control 7.8: Equipment Siting and Protection

### Exclusion Statement
This control is NOT APPLICABLE to CloudNative Analytics Ltd.

### Business Context
"Siting" implies the company physically places and positions equipment (e.g.,
servers, network hardware) within a controlled location to protect it from
environmental and physical hazards. CloudNative Analytics owns no servers,
network hardware, or infrastructure equipment of any kind — all infrastructure
is virtualized on AWS. The only physical equipment the company owns is
employee laptops, which are inherently mobile and off-premises by design,
and are addressed separately under control 7.9.

### Risk Assessment Linkage

| Physical Risk | Alternative Control | Reference |
|---|---|---|
| Environmental damage to infrastructure (fire, flood, power loss) | AWS shared responsibility model | AWS-SOC2-REVIEW |
| Damage/loss of employee equipment | 7.9 Security of assets off-premises, 7.13 Equipment maintenance | MDM-CONFIG-01, IT-ASSET-POLICY |

### Evidence of Non-Applicability
- Asset register confirming zero company-owned servers or network hardware
- AWS infrastructure diagrams confirming 100% virtualized infrastructure
- IT asset policy confirming laptops are the only physical equipment class
  the company issues, and are treated as inherently off-premises

### Auditor Anticipated Questions

Q: "Don't laptops need to be 'sited' somewhere too, like a home office?"
A: A home office is the employee's personal space, not a company-controlled
   site subject to siting/placement standards. The equipment itself is
   protected through 7.9 and 8.1, which apply regardless of physical location.

Q: "What if the company opens a physical office later?"
A: Then this exclusion would need to be re-evaluated — see below.

### Conditions for Re-evaluation
This exclusion should be re-evaluated if the company acquires any physical
infrastructure equipment (servers, networking hardware) or opens a physical
premises requiring equipment siting decisions.
